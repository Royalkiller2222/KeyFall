KeyFall - third-party components shipped with the game
=======================================================

FluidSynth 2.6.2 (lib/fluidsynth/libfluidsynth-3.dll)
  Software synthesizer that plays the soundfonts.
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 (FluidSynth-LGPL-2.1.txt in this folder)
  Source:  https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth/tree/v2.6.2
  The DLLs are the unmodified official Windows build:
  https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth/releases/tag/v2.6.2
  KeyFall loads the DLL at runtime; you may replace lib/fluidsynth/*.dll with your own
  compatible build of FluidSynth.

libsndfile (lib/fluidsynth/sndfile.dll)
  Used by FluidSynth to read compressed (SF3) soundfonts.
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 (same text as above)
  Source:  https://github.com/libsndfile/libsndfile

SDL 3 (lib/fluidsynth/SDL3.dll)
  Used by FluidSynth as a fallback audio output.
  License: zlib license - https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL/blob/main/LICENSE.txt
  Source:  https://github.com/libsdl-org/SDL

YDP Grand Piano soundfont (fluidsynth/pianos/piano.sf2)
  FreePats project, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
  https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/

certifi (_internal/certifi/cacert.pem)
  Mozilla's list of root certificates, used to verify GitHub's certificate when KeyFall
  checks for updates. Shipped unmodified.
  License: Mozilla Public License 2.0 - https://mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/
  Source:  https://github.com/certifi/python-certifi
